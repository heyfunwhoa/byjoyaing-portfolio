## Display and provenance

- UT guidance: https://umac.utexas.edu/brand-center/visual-identity/
- WGU guidance: https://www.brand.wgu.edu/logos
- Preserve the supplied complete marks, colors, proportions, and whitespace.
  Do not redraw, crop, recolor, add effects, or substitute a standalone mascot,
  shield, or academic seal. The WGU file is its current horizontal university mark.
- The SVG markup retains the official visual geometry, colors, and viewBoxes.
  Source review found no scripts, event attributes, foreign objects, embedded
  images, or external resource references.
- Store and serve the marks locally. They do not require a visitor's browser to
  contact either university or a logo service.
- About pairs each mark with the complete institution and credential text.
  WGU's MBA remains explicitly in progress. Other learning credentials remain
  text only. Logos use empty alt text because the adjacent text identifies the
  institutions; they do not add links or keyboard stops.
- About uses existing `next/image` rendering, original aspect ratios, restrained
  maximum widths, and padded clear space. WGU renders wider than its documented
  52px minimum. Marks identify education and do not imply sponsorship or endorsement.

Rights remain with the respective institutions. The portfolio's source license
and Lucide's icon license do not grant rights to these university marks. Follow
the linked institutional guidelines for future use.
