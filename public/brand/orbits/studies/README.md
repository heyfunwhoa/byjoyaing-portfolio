Do not use these compositions as portraits or imply that the nodes represent live data, projects, or people. Preserve the original files and their embedded SVG metadata.

The supplied SVGs were checked for scripts, event-handler attributes, remote references, and foreign content before they were added. The PNG is 1024 × 1024.
