## How to practice

Use the same fictional account throughout. Keep evidence confidence separate from workflow status and test result. Each lesson has an exercise, explanation check and artifact rubric. Reviewed, practiced and passed checks are separate; completing these materials does not certify professional competence. The existing Academy handles reading and knowledge checks; artifact review is manual.

## Framework boundaries

SPICED and SPIN support discovery; MEDDIC/MEDDICC/MEDDPICC support qualification; Sandler helps mutual expectations; Challenger and Command of the Message inform value conversations; BANT is a lightweight initial screen; principled negotiation and BATNA inform commercial judgment. A mutual action plan coordinates execution. These are not interchangeable or a mandatory script. Named methodologies belong to their respective owners. No proprietary training materials or private coaching documents are reproduced.

## Publication boundary

All scenario facts, numeric thresholds, organizations and results are invented for learning. No claim of customer adoption, reduced sales cycles or revenue impact is made. Keep completed real customer worksheets in the approved company workspace, not this public repository. Read source links for authoritative definitions; use counsel and designated security owners for actual approvals.
