# Transition to production or close out responsibly

[Track home](README.md) · [Scenario and templates](toolkit.md)

**Format:** Available reading lesson and manual practice. **Basis:** Original teaching synthesis using the named sources below. **Review:** Draft for editorial review; no professional certification.

## Understand

The evaluation ends with a documented decision and ownership transfer. Production readiness and adoption need their own plan.

For a go decision, transfer scope, architecture, approved exceptions, commitments, success measures and implementation responsibilities.

For a no-go or delay, revoke temporary access, handle retained data as agreed and record unresolved findings securely.

Schedule adoption and outcome reviews around the customer’s goals, not just account activity.

Document lessons learned without turning one synthetic exercise or isolated result into a universal success claim.

## See it in the fictional scenario

The production owner must acknowledge the handoff. An email sent by Sales is not evidence that monitoring, support or remediation ownership has been accepted.

## Try

Complete both a go handoff and no-go cleanup plan. State who confirms credentials are revoked and which unresolved commitments require explicit acceptance.

## Explain your decision

The buyer declines to proceed. Which task remains?

1. None
2. Agree and verify access and data cleanup with accountable owners
3. Keep credentials for a future opportunity

**Answer:** 2. No purchase does not remove responsibility for the access or data created during evaluation.
