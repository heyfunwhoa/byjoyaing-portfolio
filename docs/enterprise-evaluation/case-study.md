# From technical evaluation to buying decision

**Illustrative model · Independent portfolio project · Original synthetic materials**

## Problem

A technical evaluation can produce findings without making the purchasing decision clearer. This project explores how to connect technical tests, business outcomes, stakeholder decisions and operational handoff in one reviewable packet.

## Contribution and scope

Kristen Aing’s portfolio concept connects enterprise selling, cross-functional coordination and reusable learning materials. This implementation is AI-assisted and awaits human editorial review. It is an independent demonstration, not a record of a customer engagement or an employer-approved process. No customer, revenue, cycle-time or adoption outcomes are claimed.

## Design decisions

- Use one fictional customer and product throughout so artifacts stay consistent.
- Make test results, workflow progress, evidence confidence and buying approval separate concepts.
- Teach methodologies by the question they answer, with publisher attribution.
- Pair a buyer-facing decision packet with internal process literacy; keep internal negotiation limits out of the proposal.
- Include defer/no-go and cleanup as legitimate outcomes.
- Reuse the Academy lesson cards, navigation, prerequisite metadata and knowledge-check behavior. Artifact review stays manual.

## What this demonstrates

Problem framing, enterprise decision mapping, technical-evaluation coordination, executive communication and a repeatable review process. It demonstrates the design of artifacts; actual leadership effectiveness and commercial results require separate workplace evidence.

## Validation and limitations

The repository checks verify curriculum integration, not technical vendor support or legal correctness. No SA, customer, counsel or security reviewer has approved this package. Suggested next validation: an SA reviews technical assumptions; a legal/procurement practitioner reviews process literacy; a seller and CS reviewer complete the scenario and identify unclear handoffs. Record feedback and revisions before calling the model used in practice.
