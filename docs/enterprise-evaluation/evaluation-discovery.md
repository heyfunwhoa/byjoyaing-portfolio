# Qualify the problem and map the decision

[Track home](README.md) · [Scenario and templates](toolkit.md)

**Format:** Available reading lesson and manual practice. **Basis:** Original teaching synthesis using the named sources below. **Review:** Draft for editorial review; no professional certification.

## Understand

Before offering a POC, establish what uncertainty requires testing and whether the buyer has the time and owners to evaluate it.

Ask about the current workflow, its consequences, the desired change and the customer’s timing.

Map evaluator, champion candidate, economic buyer, security reviewer, procurement, legal and implementation owner. Roles can overlap; confirm rather than infer from title.

Record evidence as confirmed, supported, hypothesis or unknown, with a source and date.

Compare purchase with internal build, existing tools, competing initiatives and doing nothing. A demo or architecture workshop may answer the question without a POC.

## See it in the fictional scenario

The synthetic customer has an upcoming platform migration. Ask whether that event actually requires a purchase, who owns it and what happens if the evaluation is delayed.

## Try

Complete the executive brief and stakeholder map in toolkit.md. Write a proceed, defer or decline recommendation and three unanswered questions.

## Explain your decision

The seller wants a signature this month, but the customer has no event. What is the deadline?

1. A customer critical event
2. A seller target that needs to be distinguished from buyer urgency
3. A reason to invent urgency

**Answer:** 2. A seller forecast date is not customer evidence. Ask what changes for the buyer if the decision moves.

## Review your artifact

- Does it identify the decision and responsible owner?
- Can another person distinguish facts, assumptions and unknowns?
- Does each conclusion point to evidence or an explicit missing item?
- Have you documented an alternative and a condition that would change your recommendation?

Reading is **reviewed**; producing the artifact is **practiced**. A correct knowledge answer passes only that check. These are distinct from demonstrated workplace competence.
