# Deliver the executive readout and inspect buying readiness

[Track home](README.md) · [Scenario and templates](toolkit.md)

**Format:** Available reading lesson and manual practice. **Basis:** Original teaching synthesis using the named sources below. **Review:** Draft for editorial review; no professional certification.

## Understand

The readout separates what was demonstrated from what still needs a decision. A technically successful test may still be commercially unready.

Lead with the decision requested, then evidence, limitations, alternatives and owners of remaining work.

Tie each result to agreed criteria. Do not bury inconclusive or failed tests behind an overall success label.

Inspect technical, security, privacy, legal, procurement, budget and signature readiness independently.

Forecast categories follow the organization’s policy and dated buyer evidence. No universal probability follows from a POC pass.

## See it in the fictional scenario

The sample recommendation is a bounded coverage retest while procurement and security questions remain open. It is not a purchase recommendation disguised as a test report.

## Try

Use the synthetic results to write a five-minute readout and a 30-second version. Recommend conditional progression, a bounded retest or no-go; identify what would change your recommendation.

## Explain your decision

The POC passes but legal and funding are unknown. What can you report?

1. The deal is closed
2. Technical validation passed; commercial readiness remains unresolved
3. Guaranteed signature this month

**Answer:** 2. Each decision has its own evidence and authority. A technical result does not establish legal approval or available funds.

## Review your artifact

- Does it identify the decision and responsible owner?
- Can another person distinguish facts, assumptions and unknowns?
- Does each conclusion point to evidence or an explicit missing item?
- Have you documented an alternative and a condition that would change your recommendation?

Reading is **reviewed**; producing the artifact is **practiced**. A correct knowledge answer passes only that check. These are distinct from demonstrated workplace competence.
