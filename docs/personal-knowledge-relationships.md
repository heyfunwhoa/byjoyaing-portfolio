# Personal dimension of the portfolio knowledge model

**Status:** Public editorial model only. No graph database, Joy Index sync, ingestion, or automated publication.

## Identity is broader than work
The public site can describe:
- **Professional identity:** commercial leadership, GTM, cybersecurity and independent software building.
- **Values:** curiosity, authenticity, connection, growth and independence.
- **Interests and enjoyment:** electronic music, DIY and working on cars, learning woodworking, photography and art.
- **Expression:** how curiosity, making and connecting with others influence approach to work and learning.

These are independent relationships, not a claim that every personal interest must be a professional skill. Don't instrumentalize every hobby into a career achievement.

## Publishing boundaries
- The curated `lib/public-personal.ts` is approved-in-code *candidate public copy*, subject to the user's final editorial choice.
- The private Joy Index can maintain more extensive reflections, goals, emotions and contextual notes. **No direct sync to the public site.**
- Do not publish household/location patterns, intimate relationships, health, contact information of others, or personal journal entries.
- No speculative personality labels or scores. Personal strengths may be documented as self-reflection, not clinical or psychometric measurements.

## Future graph shape (not implemented)
`Person → enjoys → Interest`; `Person → values → Principle`; `Person → held → Role`; `Role → produced → Evidence`; `Person → built → Side Quest`; `Side Quest → explores → Topic`; `Person → learned → Skill`. Each public item requires a visibility choice and source/approval before it appears publicly.

## Editorial guidelines
Keep About human. Use short genuine descriptions in first person, not a hobby résumé. Let the portfolio remain primarily useful to a recruiter or founder. Add photos or music references only after confirming rights and intended privacy.
